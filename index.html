Method and simplifications

  • Geometry, profiles and grades are read from the Tekla model through the Open API. Parts are merged at shared ends and split where X-bracing crosses (connected at the crossing).
  • 3D frame with rigid joints, pinned foundations, second-order analysis (PyNite P-Δ). Design uses axial forces only, which is normal practice for bolted lattice towers. Secondary bending is not checked.
  • Angle properties from nominal dimensions without root radii (A within 1-3 % of EN 10056-1, conservative). Geometric-axis inertia is used for the frame stiffness.
  • Tension: min(A fy/γM0, net section). Legs 0.9 Anet fu/γM2; bracing connected by one leg β = 0.55 (EN 1993-1-8 3.10.3).
  • Compression: buckling curve b. Legs and chords use λeff = λ between nodes (conservative against the EN 1993-3-1 Annex G reduction). Bracing uses EN 1993-1-1 BB.1.2. For crossed diagonals, the out-of-plane length is the segment length when the partner diagonal is in tension, otherwise the full length.
  • Slenderness limits (common practice, cf. ASCE 10): legs and chords 150, bracing 200, redundant members 250, tension-only 350.
  • Partial factors are EN 1990 defaults (1.35 / 1.5), used as conservative placeholders. EN 50341-1 with the national annex uses reliability-based factors that must replace them.
  • Not covered: wind on insulators and the peak, dynamic response, fatigue, connection design beyond bolt counts, foundations design, clearances, construction and maintenance load cases, line angle and dead-end cases.
